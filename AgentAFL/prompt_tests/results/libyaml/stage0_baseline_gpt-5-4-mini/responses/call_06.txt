```yaml
---
# Anchors and aliases
defaults: &defaults
  retries: 3
  timeout: 30

service:
  <<: *defaults
  name: api
  endpoint: /v1/status
```

```yaml
---
# Nested mappings and sequences

    level2:
      - key: one
        value: 1
      - key: two
        value: 2
      - key: three
        value: 3
```

```yaml
---

```
